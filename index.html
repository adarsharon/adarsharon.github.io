A few directions
I'm thinking about.

I work on quantum field theory and conformal field theory, often where symmetry, effective field theory, and strong coupling meet. These are a few threads running through my research.

01↗

Conformal field theory

Moduli spaces & the spectrum of operators

What does a space of vacua tell us about the operators of a CFT? Large-charge methods and bootstrap constraints offer new answers.

02↗

Symmetry & defects

Beyond ordinary symmetries

Topological defects and non-invertible symmetries reveal structures that extend familiar ideas about conserved quantities.

Explore this direction →
03↗

Quantum phases

Order that survives the heat

Can a superfluid remain ordered at arbitrarily high temperature? Some quantum field theories suggest a striking yes.
