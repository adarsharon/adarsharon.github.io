01 / Strong coupling

Large charge as a window into strong coupling

Large global charge makes otherwise inaccessible observables tractable. Combining effective field theory, conformal data, and sometimes unexpected correspondences between dimensions gives a way to compute—and constrain—quantities in interacting conformal field theories.

02 / Symmetries & defects

Symmetries beyond groups

Generalized symmetries are naturally described by extended topological operators. My work considers continuous non-invertible symmetries and non-local conserved currents, the fate of non-invertible structures in string theory and gravity, and applications to quantum information.

03 / Conformal field theory

Moduli spaces & conformal data

What traces of spontaneously broken conformal symmetry are visible in the spectrum of the unbroken CFT? Theories with moduli spaces connect the operator spectrum, the physics of their vacua, and new bootstrap consistency conditions. Large-charge methods offer a particularly useful bridge between these descriptions.

Selected papers
  • Moduli Spaces in CFT: Large Charge Operatorswith Gabriel Cuomo and Leonardo Rastelli · 2024↗
  • Moduli Spaces in CFT: Bootstrap Equation in a Perturbative Examplewith Gabriel Cuomo and Leonardo Rastelli · 2024↗
04 / Thermal phases

When heat does not restore symmetry

We usually associate higher temperatures with disorder. Yet some conformal field theories support broken continuous symmetries at every nonzero temperature. These examples sharpen the relationship between entropy, order, and the possible phases of quantum fields.
